# Product philosophy

These are precision signal instruments from an alternate Finnish engineering
history: restrained, legible, durable, slightly unfamiliar, and technically
credible. They are modern tools, not replicas or nostalgia objects.

The family shares a near-black surface, off-white technical typography, strong
negative space, large musical controls, signal-driven motion, and one restrained
functional accent per product. It does not use fake materials, fasteners, rack
hardware, decorative animation, hidden essential pages, or borrowed front-panel
language.

DSP may be sophisticated; operation must remain direct. Controls name musical
phenomena and have stable, monotonic intent. Historical research informs
headroom, time constants, loading, clipping, and recovery, but never authorizes
a clone or an unsupported fidelity claim. Measurement prevents mythology;
listening prevents sterile design.

Density's opinion is controlled force. The clean parallel path preserves space
and transient life while the crush path can become hard, close, and physical.
The large Density control coordinates that behaviour without exposing the
implementation as a control panel.
