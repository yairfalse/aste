# Sound capability matrix

Baseline `8a603dc`. These are comparative capability/limitation statements,
**not numerical musical-quality ratings**. Implemented mechanisms are supported
by source; words such as useful, distinctive, expressive and preferable require
listener evidence not yet supplied for the new audit renders. The creator's
negative overall experience remains substantive evidence without being assigned
invented per-product scores.

## Sonic and dynamic capabilities

| Product | Sonic range | Character | Responsiveness | Dynamic behaviour | Depth |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Density | Dry through heavily compressed/saturated parallel output; current default already 90% wet | Opinionated compression/makeup/clip law; distinctiveness unproven | Macro coefficients move, but detector/makeup/drive interactions can offset perceived motion | Peak detector, source-level-dependent release, HPF and stereo linking | Timing and source selectivity provide real dimensions; sensitivity degeneracy needs matched tests |
| Harmonic | Broad shelves/bells; clean cuts and conditional nonlinear boosts | Bounded nonlinear state correction rather than ordinary static EQ; audibility depends on operating point | Hero is inactive when all gains are flat/cut; quiet sources reduce nonlinear participation | Serial earlier bands alter later drive; Input changes nonlinear response | EQ depth credible; nonlinear depth across source levels and shelf plateaus unproven |
| Sequence | Saw/pulse/sine morphs, detuned pair/sub, filter weighting, envelope and sequencer | Original shared-state filter, not a verified distinctive voice | Fresh keyboard notes can remain in release; macro response includes compensating gain laws | Velocity/accent post-filter; Pressure changes loading and shared contour; genuine legato/slide | Broad monophonic combinations, but one shared amplitude/filter ADSR and cutoff ceiling constrain independence |
| Loop | Capture, varispeed/reverse/pitch, motion, splice and printed generations | Genuine cumulative record/loss mechanism | Capture/print actions are explicit; current motion phase repeats the wrong one-second segment | Capture-only feedback; reproduce and print gain laws differ; generations change accumulated material | Three retained decks enable actual evolution, but pitch/speed/reverse interaction needs measurement |
| Impulse | Eight synthesized sources, broad pitch/decay ranges, driven mix | Different pulse/noise/sine/inharmonic constructions; musical separation unproven | Low Tone unused; sound controls latch on hits; shared saturation can compress differences | Per-event velocity/variation, one voice per object, tail replacement on retrigger | Rhythmic depth exceeds small per-object models; time laws and high-frequency behavior need review |

## Evidence and interpretation

[Density/Harmonic investigation](../../../research_notes/Aste%20musical%20power%20audit/density_harmonic.md),
[Sequence/Impulse investigation](../../../research_notes/Aste%20musical%20power%20audit/sequence_impulse.md),
and [Loop/Field investigation](../../../research_notes/Aste%20musical%20power%20audit/loop_field.md)
provide precise source references and separate facts from hypotheses.
[Actual editor review](INTERFACE.md) supplies the six PNGs and interaction
analysis. [Current measurements](EVIDENCE.md) determine which hypotheses have
rendered support; no source-table entry should be read as a completed musical
acceptance result.

The common limiting pattern is not simply low numerical range. It is the gap
between a gesture's apparent meaning and its actual activation, ownership,
timing or gain dependency. The instruments differ enough that one global
"more aggressive" law would obscure, rather than solve, those limits.
